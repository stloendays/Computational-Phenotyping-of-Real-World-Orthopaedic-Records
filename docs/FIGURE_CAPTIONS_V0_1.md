# Pre-gold Main-Figure Captions v0.1

These captions correspond to the manuscript assets that can be finalized before physician reference-standard results are available. Counts are derived from privacy-preserving aggregate outputs and remain explicitly labelled as deterministic audit results where clinical adjudication is pending.

## Figure 1. Validation-first computational phenotyping framework

Overview of the fixed-data clinical-informatics workflow. Eighteen heterogeneous hospital exports are reconstructed at the admission-episode level before disease/anatomy, clinical-state, procedure-relevance and multi-label procedure-component phenotypes are derived. Physician reference-standard annotation is performed within the private analytic environment, with approximately 20% independent second review. The deterministic baseline was generated and cryptographically frozen before any physician gold labels were completed; subsequent deterministic, LLM-assisted and hybrid systems are evaluated on the same locked reference standard. Only versioned code, privacy-preserving aggregate results, small-cell-suppressed outputs, figure/table generators and cryptographic manifests enter the public reproducibility layer.

## Figure 2. Cohort reconstruction and anatomy disambiguation

Admission-level reconstruction and deterministic anatomy audit across the three orthopaedic domains. **A**, diagnosis/demographics exports contain substantial repeated-row inflation, reaching 10.85, 12.40 and 13.40 rows per admission for hallux valgus, first-CMC candidate retrieval and scaphoid candidate retrieval, respectively. **B**, broad candidate retrieval is reduced to deterministic phenotype-v0.3 cohorts of 193/200 hallux-valgus, 28/79 first-CMC and 68/88 wrist-scaphoid episodes. These strict counts are algorithmic outputs pending physician confirmation. **C**, the 88 scaphoid-retrieval candidates are explicitly partitioned into 68 wrist-scaphoid, 13 foot-navicular and 7 anatomically ambiguous episodes; ambiguous records are retained for physician adjudication rather than forced into a binary class. **D**, comparison with the superseded heuristic shows that 68 of 72 previously selected wrist episodes were retained and four were removed as explicit foot-navicular records; no new wrist-scaphoid episodes were added. This transition demonstrates removal of anatomical contamination rather than sample-size expansion.

## Figure 3. Procedure attribution exposes a second hidden EHR error mode

Procedure attribution is treated as a separate task from procedure-component recognition. **A**, a single admission may contain both the target study disease and an unrelated orthopaedic problem, so generic operative terms can be incorrectly attributed to the target disease if the entire operative-note module is treated as disease-specific. The workflow therefore applies an anatomy-aware relevance gate before multi-label procedure extraction. **B**, detailed operative-note module availability exceeds target-disease procedure-note availability in hallux valgus (114 vs 113 admissions) and wrist scaphoid disease (31 vs 28), whereas all 18 first-CMC detailed-note admissions are currently disease-concordant under the deterministic audit. **C**, validation is prespecified hierarchically: procedure relevance is evaluated first; procedure components are then evaluated conditional on physician-confirmed target-disease relevance and again end-to-end so that relevance errors cannot be hidden by a high component-level F1 score. All counts shown before physician adjudication are deterministic audit outputs rather than validated clinical prevalence estimates.
