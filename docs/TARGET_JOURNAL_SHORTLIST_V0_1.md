# Target Journal Shortlist v0.1

This shortlist is based on **scope and manuscript fit**, not on chasing impact factor or quartile.

The current project is a small, focused, retrospective wrist-scaphoid treatment-composition study. It should be submitted where a clinically specific wrist question and careful retrospective methodology are valued.

## 1. Journal of Wrist Surgery — first-choice fit

### Why it fits

The journal explicitly covers wrist/carpal pathology and reconstructive surgery and identifies original scientific manuscripts as a core article type.

Its recent content includes small retrospective wrist studies, including a 2026 retrospective cohort of 25 patients undergoing a scapholunate procedure and recent scaphoid/nonunion scientific articles.

Representative sources:

- Journal of Wrist Surgery editorial scope: wrist and carpal pathology, diagnosis, treatment, reconstructive surgery and original peer-reviewed articles.
- Mack ZE, et al. *Patient Outcomes Following Reduction and Association of the Scaphoid and Lunate: A Retrospective Cohort Study*. J Wrist Surg. 2026;15(2):e21-e27. doi:10.1055/a-2537-2648.
- Holzmer SW, et al. *A Retrospective Review of Scaphoid Nonunion Treatment: Does Graft Type Impact Union Rate and Correction of Humpback Deformity?* J Wrist Surg. 2026 online/DOI:10.1055/a-2793-1422.

### Recommended framing

Lead with the clinical question:

> **Operative composition across acute/new versus established chronic/nonunion wrist-scaphoid presentations.**

Emphasize:

- physician-confirmed wrist cohort;
- fixation as a common mechanical base;
- graft augmentation as the varying component;
- duration as a small exploratory secondary analysis;
- careful handling of same-admission procedure attribution.

Do not lead with NLP, computational phenotyping, GitHub workflow or AI.

### Main risk

The operative sample may be judged small and the treatment-composition association may be considered clinically expected. The paper therefore needs a clean effect estimate, physician adjudication, transparent uncertainty and concise presentation.

**Current rank: #1.**

## 2. Journal of Hand Surgery Global Online — strong second choice

### Why it fits

The journal publishes clinical hand/wrist research and has recently published scaphoid studies using retrospective cohorts, national/real-world databases and practice-pattern designs.

Representative sources:

- Strelzow JA, et al. *Scaphoid Fractures and Nonunion: A Survey-based Review of Hand Surgeon's Practice and the Evidence*. J Hand Surg Glob Online. 2024;6(6):836-841. doi:10.1016/j.jhsg.2024.06.013.
- Kadhim M, Donohue JK, Fowler JR. *Distal Scaphoid Excision for Chronic and Nonchronic Scaphoid Fracture Nonunion*. J Hand Surg Glob Online. 2024;6(4):519-523. doi:10.1016/j.jhsg.2024.03.013.
- Phan A, et al. *Early Nonsteroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug Prescriptions and Nonunion After Scaphoid Fractures: A TriNetX Matched Cohort Study*. J Hand Surg Glob Online. 2026;8(3):100958. doi:10.1016/j.jhsg.2026.100958.

### Recommended framing

Slightly broaden the practice-pattern angle:

> **How documented operative strategy changes across physician-confirmed scaphoid presentation states in routine care.**

The methodologic elements of anatomical disambiguation and procedure attribution can receive somewhat more space here, but the clinical question should remain primary.

### Main risk

The journal has access to many larger database studies. The manuscript should therefore avoid competing on sample size and instead emphasize high-specificity clinical abstraction and operative-detail granularity that claims databases cannot provide.

**Current rank: #2.**

## 3. Hand Surgery & Rehabilitation — plausible third choice

### Why it fits

The journal publishes original articles, literature reviews, technical notes, case reports and other work related to hand disease, including traumatic and degenerative pathology. It has also published recent scaphoid nonunion graft literature, including the 2024 umbrella review.

Representative source:

- Baamir A, et al. *Graft choice for managing scaphoid non-union: umbrella review*. Hand Surg Rehabil. 2024;43(4):101759. doi:10.1016/j.hansur.2024.101759.

### Recommended framing

Keep the manuscript clinically compact and emphasize:

- scaphoid trauma/nonunion;
- documented treatment composition;
- graft-selection heterogeneity;
- careful physician review.

### Main risk

The main association may be judged too descriptive unless physician-adjudicated results show a clear separation in graft augmentation and the duration secondary analysis remains coherent.

**Current rank: #3.**

# Submission strategy

## If physician Gold preserves a strong bone-graft association

Submit first to **Journal of Wrist Surgery**.

Use a concise original scientific article with:

- 3 main figures;
- 2 main tables;
- focused Introduction;
- no generic AI/framework framing.

## If the primary association is directionally consistent but statistically fragile

Still consider Journal of Wrist Surgery or Journal of Hand Surgery Global Online, but frame the study explicitly as exploratory and emphasize exact estimates, confidence intervals and leave-one-out influence rather than binary significance.

## If physician Gold materially attenuates or reverses the deterministic signal

Do not switch the primary endpoint.

Options:

1. report the negative/measurement-sensitive result as a concise observational study;
2. consider whether the physician-adjudication/real-world cohort contamination result is independently publishable as a methods note;
3. otherwise stop the stand-alone clinical manuscript rather than manufacture a new endpoint.

---

# Manuscript format recommendation

Current best format:

- focused original article / scientific article;
- approximately 2,500-3,500 words unless target-journal instructions require otherwise;
- 3 main figures;
- Table 1 baseline characteristics;
- Table 2 operative composition/effect estimates;
- duration analysis as Figure 3 or Supplementary Table depending final sample size;
- detailed deterministic rules and audit history in Supplementary material/repository rather than the main narrative.

The target-journal choice should be revisited only after physician Gold defines the actual acute/new and chronic/nonunion operative denominators.
